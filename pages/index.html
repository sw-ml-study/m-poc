Rosetta M

One computation, three faces, and time. Each stone is one small machine-learning computation shown as its Math, the proposed M code, and its Visual (structure and live data), with time as the fourth dimension. Choose a stone above; drag it to rotate; scrub the steps and the numbers move while the equation, the code and the dataflow stay put.

M is proposed, not run. The M face is notation rendered from the same record as the Math face; nothing here parses or evaluates it, and its glyphs are placeholders. Every number on the page comes from a real training run in sw-MLPL, replayed from a recorded trace.
