Rosetta M

One computation, three faces, and time. A single neuron ŷ = wx + b fitted to four points by gradient descent: its Math, the proposed M code, and its Visual (structure and live data), on one stone. Drag to rotate it; scrub the epochs and the numbers move while the equation, the code and the dataflow stay put.

M is proposed, not run. The M face is notation rendered from the same record as the Math face; nothing here parses or evaluates it, and its glyphs are placeholders. Every number on the page comes from a real training run in sw-MLPL, replayed from a recorded trace.
