Rosetta M

One computation, three faces, and time. Each stone is one small machine-learning computation shown as its Math, the proposed M code, and its Visual (structure and live data), with time as the fourth dimension; some stones add a Trace face, the time axis itself. Choose a stone above; drag it to rotate, or lay its faces side by side; scrub the steps and the numbers move while the equation, the code and the dataflow stay put; walk the equation symbol by symbol and watch each one light up on every face. Rest the pointer on anything for an explanation; press ? for the keys.

M is proposed, not run. The M face is notation rendered from the same record as the Math face; nothing here parses or evaluates it, and its glyphs are placeholders. Every number on the page comes from a real training run in sw-MLPL, replayed from a recorded trace.

      Keys

      Blender-style modes: press a key, move the mouse, click or Enter to confirm, Esc to cancel and put things back.

      ggrab: move the stone in the stage
      rrotate: mouse left and right turns the stone, up and down tilts it
      sscale: mouse up grows the view, down shrinks it
      ttravel: mouse left and right scrubs the epoch; ← → step one
      ffocus: the next click picks a symbol; f again or Esc clears
      1 2 3 4turn to the Math, M, Visual and Trace faces (4 only on a stone with a Trace face)
      Enterread the face toward you (and confirm a mode)
      wside by side: every face flat in a row; w or Esc returns to the stone
      n pwalk: the next / previous line or symbol of the equation, lit on every face; Esc stops
      Spaceplay / pause the epochs
      aauto-rotate on / off
      Home 0reset the view: scale, position, angle
